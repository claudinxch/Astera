# Astera

A study-management app for university students. Students organise their academic life into Programs, Courses and Tasks, track time spent studying, and receive AI-assisted daily plans.

## Language

### Structure

**Program**:
A named workspace that groups a student's courses. Corresponds loosely to a degree or area of study. A student may have multiple programs (e.g. "BSc Computer Science", "Side Learning").
_Avoid_: Degree, workspace, context

**Term**:
A named period of time within a Program that contains a set of Courses (e.g. "Fall 2026"). Has a start date and an end date. Optional on a Program — informal programs may have no terms.
_Avoid_: Semester, quarter, period

**Course**:
A specific enrollment in a subject during a particular Term (e.g. "Calculus I — Fall 2026"). Not an abstract course definition: retaking a subject means creating a new Course record.
_Avoid_: Class, subject, module

### Academic tasks

**Assignment**:
A piece of graded work within a Course with a due date, an importance rating, and an estimated effort. Types: homework, project, test, lab, reading, other.
_Avoid_: Task, deliverable, submission

**Test** *(assignment type)*:
An assignment type for a short graded in-class assessment.
_Avoid_: Quiz (reserved for Practice Quiz, the self-study learning tool)

**Exam**:
A formal, high-stakes graded assessment within a Course. Has a date, an optional weight toward the final grade, and an importance rating used for prioritisation. Status `completed` means the student sat the exam — not that they finished studying for it.
_Avoid_: Final, assessment

**Importance**:
A 1–5 self-rated field on Assignments and Exams indicating how much the item matters for the student's grade and goals. 1 = minor (barely counts), 3 = moderate (default, normal graded work), 5 = critical (major exam or project that could make or break the course). Used as a signal in the scoring engine.
_Avoid_: Priority (that's the output of the engine, not an input), weight (that's the grade percentage)

**Course Difficulty**:
A 1–5 self-rated field on a Course indicating how much cognitive effort the course demands from this student personally. Entirely subjective. 1 = easy for me, 5 = very demanding for me. Used by the scoring engine to push harder courses up in the Daily Plan.
_Avoid_: Complexity, level

### Grades

**Score**:
The numeric result a student received on an Assignment or Exam, stored internally as 0–100 regardless of the program's grading scale.
_Avoid_: Grade (overloaded — see Course Grade), mark, result

**Course Grade**:
The computed or manually overridden grade for a Course, derived from the weighted or simple average of its Scores.
_Avoid_: Final grade, GPA (GPA is a cross-program concept, not per-course)

**Weight**:
The percentage of the Course Grade a given Assignment or Exam contributes. Optional; when absent, the Course Grade falls back to a simple average.
_Avoid_: Percentage, contribution

### Study

**Study Session**:
A recorded block of time a student spent studying, always linked to a Course and optionally to a specific Assignment or Exam. Has a study method and a duration in focus minutes.
_Avoid_: Session, focus block, pomodoro (that's a method, not the session itself)

**Study Method**:
The time-structuring technique used during a Study Session. Supported methods: Pomodoro, 52/17, 90/20 (ultradian), Flowtime, Custom.
_Avoid_: Technique, mode, timer

### Notes and learning tools

**Note**:
A text document (markdown) created by the student, always linked to a Course and optionally to a specific Assignment or Exam. Can be authored manually or generated from a photo of handwritten notes.
_Avoid_: Document, annotation

**Flashcard Deck**:
A collection of Flashcards generated from a single Note by the AI.
_Avoid_: Deck, card set

**Flashcard**:
A front/back pair generated from a Note, used for self-study.
_Avoid_: Card

**Practice Quiz**:
A set of multiple-choice questions generated from a Note or an Exam's topics by the AI, used for self-study. Not a graded academic submission.
_Avoid_: Quiz (the assignment type is "test"), test (the assignment type)

### Planning

**Daily Plan**:
A prioritised list of up to 5 Assignments and Exams recommended for a student to focus on today, generated by the scoring engine and shaped by the AI into a schedule with time blocks and explanations. Database tables: `daily_plans` / `daily_plan_items`.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, daily recommendation

**Priority Score**:
A numeric score computed by the scoring engine for each Assignment or Exam, used to rank them in the Daily Plan. Factors: urgency, importance, course difficulty, estimated hours, study time already logged.
_Avoid_: Score (overloaded — see Score under Grades)
